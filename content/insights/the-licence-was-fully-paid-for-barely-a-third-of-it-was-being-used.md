# The Situation

Ashgrove Insurance Services had licensed Objectif Lune's full capability several years earlier, with genuine plans to use it broadly across document and communications workflows. Over time, without anyone deciding it deliberately, usage had narrowed to a single purpose: generating static policy documents. As a renewal approached, Ashgrove's finance team began asking a reasonable question — did the licence cost still make sense, given how little of it appeared to be in active use?

# The Estate Review

Rather than treating the renewal as a simple cost decision, we ran a structured review of the full licensed capability against what was actually being used:

- A capability audit, listing every feature included in Ashgrove's licence tier
- Usage data pulled directly from the platform, showing which capabilities were active and which had never been configured at all
- Conversations with operational teams across the business to identify manual processes that overlapped with unused platform capability

# What the Review Found

Ashgrove was using roughly a third of what they were already paying for. Dynamic customer communications, automated compliance archiving with built-in audit trail, and multi-channel output — all included in the existing licence, none of it configured or active. Meanwhile, a compliance team elsewhere in the business was manually compiling and archiving records for audit purposes, a process the platform could already do automatically.

# The Rollout

We built a phased adoption plan prioritising the two capabilities offering the clearest, fastest return — starting with compliance archiving, given the scale of manual effort it would replace — and supported the configuration and rollout directly with Ashgrove's team.

# The Result

Within four months, the compliance archiving process that had previously consumed roughly fifteen hours of manual admin time each week was fully automated, using capability Ashgrove had been paying for the entire time. The renewal conversation shifted from "can we justify this cost?" to "what should we activate next?"

# The Lesson

Before questioning the cost of an established platform licence, it's worth checking, properly, what it already includes. Under-used capability is a common and often significant source of hidden value — and finding it is usually far cheaper than replacing the platform.
