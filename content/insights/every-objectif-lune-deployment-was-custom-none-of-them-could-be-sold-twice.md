# The situation

Black Dog Financial Print had built a genuinely strong reputation delivering Objectif Lune implementations for financial services clients. Every project was treated, and delivered, as bespoke — understandable, given the sensitivity and regulatory context of the sector. The consequence was a sales process that started from a blank page every time, with long cycles built around convincing each new prospect that a custom solution could be designed for their specific needs.

# Finding the pattern

We reviewed the technical detail ofBlack Dog's last dozen delivered projects, looking specifically for what was genuinely different between them versus what simply looked different on the surface. A clear pattern emerged: roughly 70% of every implementation, across otherwise very different clients, was solving the same core problem — generating regulatory statements with a compliant, embedded audit trail, adapted only in surface detail to each client's specific regulatory reporting format.

# Packaging the repeatable core

Rather than continuing to treat every project as fully bespoke, we packaged the common 70% into a named, defined use case:

- A fixed core scope, covering the regulatory statement generation and audit trail functionality common to every prior project
- A standard implementation timeline, based on genuine delivery data from past projects rather than a fresh estimate each time
- Transparent, tiered pricing for the packaged core, with a clearly scoped and separately priced allowance for the remaining, genuinely client-specific 30%

# The result

Sales cycles for the newly packaged use case shortened by roughly 40%. Prospects were no longer being asked to imagine and trust an entirely bespoke solution from a standing start — they were being shown a proven, named offering with a track record of successful delivery behind it, alongside a clear and honest scope for what remained genuinely custom to them.

# The lesson

Bespoke delivery, however excellent, is hard to sell efficiently because every prospect has to be convinced afresh. Finding and packaging the genuine common core underneath a series of custom projects — while being honest about what remains truly custom — turns strong delivery capability into a repeatable, scalable proposition.
